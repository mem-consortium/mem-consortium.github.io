About MEMC

Official name

mem-consortium.org

Short description

A research community fostering collaboration among scholars, researchers, and practitioners dedicated to advancing the understanding and development of emerging markets.

Logo Use Guidelines

  • Use the complete logo without stretching, recoloring, or changing its proportions.
  • Leave clear space around the logo so it remains legible.
  • Use the standard logo on white or very light backgrounds, and the white-lettered version on dark backgrounds.
  • Link the logo to mem-consortium.org when displaying it on another website.
  • Do not imply MEMC endorsement of an organization, event, or product without permission.
